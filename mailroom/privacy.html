Mailroom privacy policy

Mailroom is a personal tool operated by the account owner. With the owner's authorization, it accesses Gmail messages, labels and settings to review and organize the owner's mail. It may store credentials, message metadata, configuration and operation logs in private local or hosted storage used by the owner.

Mail content needed for a briefing or classification may be processed by OpenAI services selected by the owner. Google processes requests to Gmail and Google Cloud. Mailroom is not offered to other users and does not sell Google user data.

The owner can revoke the tool's Google Account access through Google Account security settings. The owner manages retention and deletion of the tool's stored data. Contact: github.com/mpeter.
